RUEN

My turn 手番

Start. Life — the number in the character's heart. Victory points — 4. 7 cards in hand for everyone. The Shogun opens each round.

1 · Start of turn

No more than 9 cards in hand — discard the rest.
Been dead? Instead: recovery — discard as many as you like, draw back up to 7. Killed by poison — no recovery.

2 · Actions, in any order

  • up to two attacks with a weapon
  • actions and group actions
  • a trap in front of you — one, face down, figure on top
  • a stance — once per turn, works right away
  • effects on others, an aura in front of you — one per player
  • stance, aura, trap: a new one replaces the old, the old one returns to hand
  • trade: 2 cards of one type → 1 new; 2 identical → 2 new

3 · End of turn

Poisoned — −2 life. Then draw: 3 cards from the deck (smaller team +1), after which every opponent draws 1.

Order of resolution

hero→ stance→ aura→ poison→ effects→ weapon→ modif.

Permanent effect before one-shot. The defender's chain is the same, ending with the trap. A «no less than 1» floor holds from where it appears; base power 0 deals 0.

End of the game: the deck runs out (the turn of the last card is the last one) · someone has 0 points · by agreement — finish the round up to the player before the Shogun.

Attack and defense 攻守

Target complexity

by default for any player, wherever they sit. A weapon reaches the target if its number ≥ the complexity. Thrown weapons reach anyone and never wake a trap. Stances, effects and auras shift complexity either way — counted when the attack is declared.

Power — how many wounds the target takes. No weapon — fists: complexity 1, power 1, costs both attacks. One attack — one modifier.

The defender picks one

  • take wounds = attack power;
  • defense from hand — attack blocked, attacker's bonuses do not fire;
  • the trap, if one lies in front and you are not defending.

Allies may add defense as an intervention: one card — wounds down to 1, two from the team — down to 0; those cards' special effects do not fire.

If you cannot defend yourself, allies cannot defend you either.

Thrust

Wounds directly: not an attack — no defense, does not wake a trap, does not spend an attack. Finish someone with a thrust — the point is yours.

Death

Life 0 — you are dead until the end of this turn: point to the killer (from poison — to the discard); stance, trap, effects and aura — to the discard. Nobody touches the dead — no attacks, no cards. From the next turn you are alive and can be hit; at the start of your own turn — recovery.

Everything else 其他

Poison

Three afflictions: at the end of your turn −2 life; a poisoned attack on you +1 wound; killed by poison — no recovery, the hand stays as it was. Poison does not affect attack power.

Trap

Fires when you are attacked and do not defend; works even if the attack is lethal. Planted something that is not a trap — you die, point to the initiator. A revealed trap lies face up and keeps working.

Effects

To the right of the character, face up. Permanent — until death, one-shot — until they fire. No two of the same name. Living players only.

Intervention

Played outside your turn — in the middle of someone's action or in a lull. Opens a transaction: everyone lays down what they want, all of it counts as simultaneous. Whoever's life is at stake picks the order; disputes go their way. Bargaining is allowed.

Aura

Affects everyone at the table, enemies included. One per player, a team's auras stack. Lives as long as its owner does.

Conditions on cards

At death's door — 1 life; At full health — the heart's maximum. Samurai / Ninja — the Shogun plays as a samurai. for each — count the table when the card is played. All other conditions — as written on the card.
