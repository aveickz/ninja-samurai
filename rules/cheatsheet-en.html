RUEN

My turn 手番

Start. Life — the number in your character's heart. Victory points — 4. The Shogun opens the round.

1 · Draw

3 cards from the deck (smaller team +1).
Bled out — recovery instead of drawing: discard as many as you like, draw up to 7.

2 · Actions, in any order

  • up to two attacks with weapons
  • actions and group actions
  • a trap in front of you — one, face down, with the figure; may be swapped
  • a stance — once per turn, the new one replaces the old, works at once
  • effects on others, an aura in front of you — one per player
  • trade: 2 cards of one type → 1 new; 2 identical → 2 new

3 · End of turn

Hand of 8 or fewer.
Poisoned — you lose 1 life.

Order of resolution

hero→ stance→ aura→ poison→ effects→ weapon→ modif.

Permanent effects before one-shot ones. Same for the defender, the trap comes last. A “not below 1” floor holds from the step where it appears; base power 0 gives 0.

End of game: the deck runs out (the turn the last card is drawn is the last one) · someone hits 0 points · by agreement — finish the round up to the player before the Shogun.

Attack and defense 攻守

Target complexity

— for any player by default, wherever they sit. A weapon reaches the target if its number ≥ the complexity. Thrown reaches anyone and does not wake a trap. Stances, effects and auras move complexity both ways — counted when the attack is declared.

Power — how many wounds the target takes. No weapon — fists: complexity 1, power 1, costs both attacks. Per attack — one modifier.

The defender picks one

  • take wounds = attack power;
  • a defense from hand — attack blocked, the attacker's bonuses do not fire;
  • the trap, if one is out and you are not defending.

An ally may add one defense as an intervention: −1 wound, its special effects do not work.

Thrust

Wounds straight through: not an attack — no defense, does not wake a trap, does not spend an attack. Finish someone with a thrust — the point is yours.

Death

Life 0 — you have bled out: a point to the killer (from poison — to the discard); stance, trap, effects and aura go to the discard. You cannot be attacked, but your trap can be interacted with. You get back up on your own turn through recovery.

Everything else 其他

Poison

Three ailments: your attack power −1 (but not below 1); a poisoned attack on you +1 wound; at end of turn −1 life. Poison cancels stance bonuses.

Trap

Fires when you are attacked and do not defend; works even against a lethal attack. Planted something that is not a trap — you die, the point goes to the initiator. Enlightened — face up, keeps working.

Effects

To the right of the character, face up. Permanent — until death, one-shot — until they fire. No two of the same name. Only on the living.

Intervention

Played outside your turn — mid-action or in silence. Opens a transaction: everyone lays down what they want, all of it counts as simultaneous. Whoever's life is at stake picks the order; disputes go their way. Bargaining is allowed.

Aura

Affects everyone at the table, enemies too. One per player, a team's auras stack. Lives as long as its owner.

Condition badges

At death's door — 1 life; At full health — the heart's maximum. Samurai / Ninja — the Shogun plays as a samurai. If you are poisoned. “for each” — count what is on the table. While in a stance / In stance X — do not work under poison.
