# Samurai vs Ninja — table cheat sheet

English version of `cheatsheet.md`. A translation, not the original: the
Russian text is the source of truth, this file follows it. Draft copy for a
landscape A5 sheet, three columns, one block each. Layout — `cheatsheet-en.html`.
Rules source — `rules/rules-en.html` (itself translated from `rules.html`).

---

## Column 1 — My turn

**Start.** Life — the number in your character's heart. Victory points — 4.
The Shogun opens the round.

**1. Draw** — 3 cards from the deck (smaller team +1).
If you have bled out — **recovery** instead of drawing: discard as many as you
like, then draw up to 7.

**2. Actions, in any order:**
- ⚔ up to **two attacks** with weapons
- action cards and group action cards
- a trap in front of you — **one**, face down, with the figure; may be swapped
- a stance — **once per turn**, the new one replaces the old, works at once
- effects on others, an aura in front of you (**one** per player)
- trade: 2 cards of one type → 1 new; 2 identical → 2 new

**3. End of turn** — hand of **8 or fewer**.
Poisoned — you lose **1 life**.

---

## Column 2 — Attack and defense

**Target complexity** — 1 for any player by default, wherever they sit.
A weapon reaches the target if its number ≥ the complexity. **Thrown** reaches
anyone and **does not wake a trap**. Stances, effects and auras move complexity
both ways. No weapon — fists: complexity 1, power 1, costs **both** attacks.
One attack — **one modifier** (red band).

**The defender picks one:**
- take wounds = attack power;
- a **defense** from hand — attack blocked, attacker's bonuses do not fire;
- the **trap**, if one is out and you are not defending.

An ally may add **one** defense as an intervention: −1 wound, its special
effects do not work.

**Thrust** ⚡ — wounds straight through: not an attack, no defense, does not
wake a trap, does not spend an attack. Finish someone with a thrust — the point
is yours.

**Death.** Life 0 — you have bled out: a victory point to the killer (from poison
— to the discard); stance, trap, effects and aura go to the discard. You cannot
be attacked, but your trap can be interacted with. You get back up on your own
turn through recovery.

---

## Column 3 — Everything else

**Poison** ☠ — three ailments: your attack power −1 (but not below 1); a
poisoned attack on you +1 wound; at end of turn −1 life. Poison **cancels stance
bonuses**.

**Trap** — fires when you are attacked and do not defend; works even against a
lethal attack. Planted something that is not a trap — you die, the point goes to
the initiator. An enlightened trap lies face up and keeps working.

**Effects** — to the right of the character, face up. Permanent ones last until
death, one-shot ones until they fire. No two of the same name. Only on the living.

**Intervention** 🌀 — played **outside your turn**, in the middle of someone
else's action or in silence. Opens a transaction: everyone lays down what they
want, all of it counts as simultaneous. Whoever's life is at stake **picks the
order** — disputes go their way. Bargaining is allowed.

**Aura** — affects everyone at the table, enemies too. One per player, a team's
auras stack. Lives as long as its owner.

**Condition badges:** *At death's door* — 1 life · *At full health* — the
heart's maximum · *Samurai* / *Ninja* — the Shogun plays as a samurai ·
*If you are poisoned* · "for each" — count what is on the table · *While in a
stance* / *In stance X* — do not work under poison.

**Order of resolution** — from permanent to temporary:
character → stance → aura → poison → permanent effect → one-shot effect →
weapon → modifier; the same for the defender, the trap comes last. A "not below
1" floor holds from the step where it appears; a base power of 0 gives 0.

**End of game:** the deck runs out (the turn the last card is drawn is the last
one) · someone hits 0 points · by agreement — finish the round up to the player
before the Shogun.
